# Global Diagnosis Exam

A chairside teaching tool for the Global Diagnosis system of Robbins and Rouse (4 global
diagnoses, 5 CORE questions, 6 tools). Enter the exam measurements and the page names the global
diagnoses, primary and secondary treatments and sequencing considerations live, then builds a
plain-text note to paste into the chart.

**Use it:** https://rarcemunoz.github.io/global-diagnosis/ (address confirmed after first deploy)

- Nothing is saved or sent. Everything runs in your browser, and refreshing clears it.
- Do not enter patient names or chart numbers.
- Clinical judgment governs. The decision rules operationalise the book and are listed on the Reference tab.

Source: Robbins JW, Rouse JS. *Global Diagnosis: A New Vision of Dental Diagnosis and Treatment
Planning.* Quintessence; 2016. This repository contains no text from the book.
